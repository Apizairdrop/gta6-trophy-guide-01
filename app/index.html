    Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

    Work in this order. The story comes first because there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus: a missed story trophy means a new playthrough, so the manual-save rule below is the whole roadmap in one line.

      The one rule that saves the platinum

      Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter, and one more before the final mission. With no mission replay, that save is your only way back to a missed story trophy. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they disable trophies and saving. (Sources: Output Lag — GTA 6 trophy list guide; PowerPyx.)

      Missable trophies —

      Every trophy below can be missed in a single playthrough, because the game has no mission replay. The worst of them is Positive Mental Attitude: Lucia's balcony meditation spot in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter. Do the Chapter 1 meditation before Chapter 1 ends.
